**[title] hook**
Google just raised the price of their flagship foldable to $1,899, and almost immediately started sending out secret 30% discount codes. By the end of this video, you'll know exactly whether the Pixel 11 lineup is worth your money, or if you should wait for the price to drop even further.

**[product] lineup-overview**
The full family consists of the standard Pixel 11, the 11 Pro, the 11 Pro XL, and the headlining Pixel 11 Pro Fold. All of them are running Google's new Tensor G6 processor, built specifically around Gemini AI intelligence.

**[stat] fold-pricing**
But the Fold is where things get controversial. It starts at $1,899 for 256GB of storage—a $100 price hike over last year without giving you a single extra gigabyte out of the box.

**[screenshot] early-discounts**
Yet within weeks of launch, Google quietly sent 30% promo codes to Google AI Pro subscribers. That cuts up to $570 off the Fold and knocks $250 to $390 off the regular Pro models.

**[broll] crease-hardware**
So what are you actually buying? Google gave the Fold a redesigned gearless hinge with a larger bend radius. But if you were hoping for a crease-free screen, you won't find it here; the fold is still clearly visible head-on and unmistakable under your thumb.

**[quote] competitor-context**
In fact, reviewers weren't shy about calling Google out. While rivals like Samsung, Oppo, and Honor push ultra-thin chassis and silicon-carbon cells, Google's design feels stubbornly thick and boxy.

**[compare] battery-dimensions**
Google did shave off 19 grams and roughly 1 millimeter of thickness. But to do that without silicon-carbon tech, they actually shrank the battery down to 4,806mAh. You'll still get through a standard day, but don't expect a two-day battery.

**[product] display-durability**
On the bright side, the displays are genuinely stunning. Both inner and outer screens peak at 3,600 nits, making outdoor glare a non-issue. Plus, with full IP68 water resistance and a glass-fiber composite back, this is one of the toughest foldables built.

**[broll] hilight-feature**
Then there's HiLight—a notification LED ring wrapped around the camera visor. It sounds cool until you realize it only triggers when your phone is face-down on a desk with the screen off, limited to just five colors and favorite contacts.

**[list] software-features**
Where Google shines is software. Android 17 introduces Bubbles, docking background apps in the lower corner for easy multitasking. And their new sign-to-text accessibility feature translates American Sign Language straight through the front camera in real time.

**[broll] camera-system**
The cameras remain standard Pixel excellence. You get a larger 48-megapixel main sensor, up to 30x Super Zoom on the telephoto, and improved Magic Capture processing. It's reliable, sharp, and effortless to shoot with.

**[title] verdict**
Here's the bottom line. The Pixel 11 family has great displays, solid cameras, and top-tier durability. But charging $1,899 for a foldable with an obvious crease and a shrunken battery is a tough sell. Don't pay full price—wait for the discounts that Google is already sending out.
