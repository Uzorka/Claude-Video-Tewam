**[title] hook**
Tim Cook has officially stepped down as Apple CEO, and Apple is suddenly letting Google and LG power core pieces of its ecosystem. This is the single biggest identity shift in Cupertino in fifteen years.

**[stat] payoff**
By the end of this video, you will know whether Apple's new AI architecture and smart home line actually justify its massive 36 times earnings multiple.

**[timeline] succession**
Tim Cook delivered his final WWDC address in June, formally passing the CEO title to hardware engineering lead John Ternus in September. Ternus takes over right as Apple scrambles to catch up in agentic AI.

**[product] siri_ai**
The frontline of that fight is Siri AI. It is a completely redesigned assistant with a standalone app, long conversational recall, screen context awareness on Mac, and autonomous agents in the Passwords app.

**[process] ai_pipeline**
Instead of building frontier models strictly in-house, Apple partnered with Google. Lightweight queries process on your device, while heavy tasks route to AFM Cloud Pro, running on Nvidia GPUs inside Google Cloud.

**[list] ai_catch**
There is a massive catch. Siri AI requires Apple's latest hardware silicon to run. And due to fierce regulatory barriers, Apple had to exclude both Europe and China from the rollout.

**[specs] home_hardware**
Then comes Apple's new Home Collection. Rather than building hardware alone, Apple teamed up with LG and Schneider Electric to ship connected cameras, doorbells, and Ultra Wideband smart locks this December.

**[process] home_process**
Setup takes a single tap with your iPhone. The gear streams up to two point five K resolution with on-device detection, local storage fallback during outages, and ten days of encrypted video search on iCloud Plus.

**[screenshot] broadcom_deal**
To guarantee its domestic supply chain, Apple also signed a multiyear deal with Broadcom exceeding thirty billion dollars to build fifteen billion custom chips and filters in Fort Collins, Colorado.

**[compare] rd_vs_profit**
Services brought in thirty point seven billion dollars last quarter at seventy-six percent gross margins. But quarterly research and development spending jumped by two point nine billion, actually outpacing the Services unit's gross profit gains.

**[compare] valuation_gap**
That spending pressure makes Apple's valuation noticeable. Apple trades at thirty-six times forward earnings, compared to hardware rivals like Samsung at just five times forward earnings.

**[quote] privacy_irony**
Meanwhile, Apple's privacy marketing took a hit this year as checks went out for the ninety-five million dollar Lopez settlement, resolving claims that Siri recorded private conversations without user consent.

**[title] verdict**
John Ternus is abandoning old Apple isolationism to move quickly. By leaning on Google for cloud AI and LG for home security, Apple is trading exclusivity for raw speed.
